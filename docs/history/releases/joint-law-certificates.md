## Question

Bellman asked whether a small exact reference could construct and independently check finite
joint-law compatibility, incompatibility, conditional query bounds, and decision-relative
identification while preserving exact-versus-outer and unfinished distinctions.

## What we tried

The additive revision specified rational primal witnesses, dual optimality bounds, Farkas
infeasibility certificates, a positive-mass linear-fractional conditioning transform and inverse,
and complete common/strict action sets. A bounded producer and separate consumer exercised the
integrated uncertain-posterior example, compatible gluing, incompatible triangle, changed questions,
outer relaxations, impossible events, and missing-computation paths. Adversarial review then probed
exactness, caller mutation, and shortcut validation.

## What worked

The finite profile returned checkable exact witnesses and bounds, kept nonempty outer relaxations
from proving original-family feasibility, and treated absent certificates as unfinished. The
conditioning example preserved both a varying posterior and a uniform action. Nineteen fixed module
cases ran normally and with `-O`; original author results were reproduced where source was actually
available.

## What failed or was falsified

The first active reference accepted some false witnesses through floating-point arithmetic, exposed
caller-owned mutable constraints, and allowed incomplete task validation on shortcut routes. Those
were implementation defects, not counterexamples to the stated mathematics. The repaired reference
requires exact witness types, immutable nested subjects, and complete validation before conclusions.

## What stopped or closed

The repair did not reopen the substrate review, replace frozen results, or broaden the module into a
general solver. Missing recovery-packet sources remained explicitly unavailable, and no recovered
artifact was fabricated or relabeled as executed.

## What survived

Exact feasible family, outer approximation, incompatibility, nonidentification, impossible
conditioning, and unfinished computation remained distinct. Producer/consumer separation,
certificate checking at point of use, and byte-identified historical evidence became reusable
engineering boundaries for later work.

## Known gaps

The bounded proposer was not a general LP or nonlinear solver. Nonlinear optimization, scalable
certificate discovery, certified numerical radii, empirical-premise validation, independent
authorship, and formal verification remained outside scope. Five packet-reported recovered original
files and the separate v1.1 harness were not accessible; their expected hashes were not treated as
verified present artifacts.

# v0.0.7 — Decisions, evidence, and credible promises

Bellman is a research programme for making consequential decisions easier to inspect
and correct. It keeps a simple distinction: what we assume about the world is separate
from what follows from those assumptions.

Since v0.0.6, Bellman has grown from checking decisions under uncertainty from one
stream of observations to checking several evidence streams, whole decision sequences,
safety constraints, missing causal information, and strategic interactions. These are
bounded mathematical tools, not a general system for deciding what people should do.

A simple example is a proposed agreement backed by a bond. A participant can reveal
or withhold evidence; the other side responds; later, someone may benefit from breaking
the agreement. Bellman can check whether the proposed behavior remains worthwhile at
every decision point under each supplied model. It can also check whether the proposed
beliefs after unexpected behavior can arise from one consistent account of small mistakes.
That still does not tell us whether the supplied model describes real people.

One important finding is that two histories can recommend the same action and have the
same immediate decision values, yet cannot safely be treated as the same history.
The sender may need to retain a diagnostic distinction to explain how evidence could
be withheld consistently across different models. In the checked example, the receiver
can use less information if the sender retains that distinction and the receiver's
beliefs are rebuilt accordingly.

Progress across the four strategic directions is concrete but uneven:

- **Credibility under uncertainty:** check a specified arrangement throughout a finite
  interaction under every supplied model, including reactions to unexpected behavior.
- **Strategic disclosure:** represent genuine evidence and withholding, and identify
  information that matters to the likelihood of disclosure even when current incentives agree.
- **History and representation:** prove one safe reduction for identical continuation
  copies, and a stronger obstruction for genuinely different diagnostic histories,
  together with a restricted repair.
- **Mechanism composition:** check a supplied bond and monitoring arrangement and expose
  how averaging history-dependent monitoring can hide an incentive to defect. A general
  theory of composing or optimizing mechanisms is still open.

The causal work answers a separate question: when some outcomes have not been observed
under particular choices, what decisions remain justified across all allowed completions?
Under a stated narrow model, Bellman can sometimes identify a best decision despite
missing information; otherwise it can report that the preferred decision depends on
what is missing. A deliberately chosen cautious decision rule is kept separate from
identification. The causal and strategic components now coexist and pass combined
acceptance; this does not prove a bridge from causal observations to strategic beliefs,
payoffs or monitoring assumptions.

This remains a **research pre-release**. It does not establish empirical truth, replace
human judgment, authorize an action, or provide a production-ready decision system.

## Mathematical scope and reproducibility

This release retains analytical proofs and exact executable references. It includes
no new proof-assistant formalization and no empirical validation. Perfect recall,
information access, evidence feasibility, utilities, causal assumptions and probability
laws are supplied premises wherever the component requires them.

| Component | Exact earned scope and boundary |
|---|---|
| Statistical and sequential uncertainty | Fixed-registry multistream coverage and exact affine decisions; pathwise multi-affine rectangle/corner composition; persistent whole-policy uncertainty and bounded unsafe-set constraints. Sampling and safety premises remain supplied. |
| Causal decisions | Finite adjustment and response-type identification; two-stage policy-specific g-formula versus stronger full-subject support; an exact saturated `[0,1]^k` fibre for at most four missing binary second-stage rows, with at most 16 corners and 32 deterministic policies for the proved multi-affine queries. Corners are not the continuous fibre for arbitrary queries. |
| Sequential credibility | Full continuation deviations for one supplied assessment in a finite perfect-recall game family; a common rational power witness checked by independent polynomial and path calculations. Zero gain plus that witness yields sequential equilibrium separately in each model with shared consistency, not an ambiguity-preference equilibrium or complete consistency search. |
| Public-copy reduction | Identical positive continuation copies with an invariant specified profile and assessment preserve local values and common consistency in both directions. Global tag-contingent strategies are not all preserved. |
| Diagnostic obstruction and repair | In the binary null-disclosure class, interior posterior odds require one common limiting sender ratio across models. The retained target requires both `3/5` and `5/3`, ruling out every common consistency sequence for that assessment despite equal local values and separate modelwise witnesses. Keeping sender information permits receiver-only reduction with a witness-induced full assessment; no arbitrary-belief or reverse transport claim. |

Decisive counterexamples retain inconsistent off-path assessments, individually valid
models without a common sequence, erased coordination, later information-dependent
incentives, monitoring averaged across histories, and nonlinear queries whose extrema
are missed by corners. General strategic history reduction, broader strategy-class
transport, approximate off-path consistency, mechanism optimization, wider causal
restrictions and empirical applicability remain open.

## Machine-readable release identity

The downloadable `bellman-v0.0.7-manifest.json` identifies the exact released commit,
accepted PR head, component proofs/receipts and their SHA-256 identities, recorded
source commits, validation commands and limitations. It is generated from the released
Git objects and reuses their existing receipts. Its own digest is not embedded inside
it, avoiding circular hashing. The retained note is a publication template: the release
builder replaces `{{RELEASE_COMMIT}}` with the verified merge commit in the published text.

# Finite measurement-to-decision menu

2026-09-09. Committed research transition; no published release or tag.

A finite Bayesian menu now compares forced observation risks plus costs against
no measurement, using one persistent supplied state. The existing exact Decision
Lab observation engine is reused read-only. An AFY-motivated stipulated example
shows a useful conditional forecast losing to utility information; an informative
weaker report has zero gross decision value. A second maintenance example uses
the same interface. No empirical fitting or validation was added.

Explicit derivations, independently enumerated policies, producer-disabled local
receiving and rejection controls accompany the results. Full donor execution
requires the private repository; hosted public arithmetic checks say so. Existing
AFY, FSD and mathematical evidence is preserved and replayed at its original
edition by additive acceptance. These results do not repair AFY nonidentification,
establish a robust-prior optimum or prove strategic elicitation incentives.

See research/measurement_decision/{SPEC,FINDINGS,SOURCES,REVIEW}.md and
verification/measurement_acceptance/README.md for scope and receiving commands.

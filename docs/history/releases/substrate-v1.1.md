## Question

After the experiment closures, Bellman asked which mathematical objects, assumptions, guarantees,
and composition rules were needed to make consequential decision knowledge inspectable, reusable,
and correctable without confusing mathematical consequence with empirical truth or authority.

## What we tried

The substrate assembled seven components spanning compatible probability models, sequential
decisions, exact representations, approximation/regret, information comparison, persistent model
uncertainty, and a distinct causal interface. An adversarial review tested the contract. The v1.1
amendment clarified the reviewed boundaries, and the initial build-out developed typed partial
composition, constructive compatibility and representation procedures, sequential bounds,
persistent uncertainty, statistical coverage, and scoped extensions.

## What worked

The amendment made exact compatible-model fibers distinct from outer enclosures, restored the
premises needed by approximation bounds, required executable total policies across covered support,
separated model, calculation, and coverage errors, and made dependency/revision obligations
explicit. The build-out supplied a coherent constructive agenda and 17 portable illustrative
mathematical calculation groups reproduced from the frozen record.

## What failed or was falsified

The review defeated any reading of the first substrate as a completed or implementation-ready
mathematical contract. Exact versus outer families, support, signed losses, numerical-error margins,
decoder constructibility, and the engineering correspondence needed clarification. The archive
could not support claims that the original v1 or its reported fixed harness had been reproduced.

## What stopped or closed

No new experiment was opened. Retired KL methods and the observation-compression branch remained
closed. The substrate did not authorize a Writ architecture change, a new ontology, or a claim that
the seven-component structure was final.

## What survived

Typed conditional statements, explicit assumptions, exact/outer separation, support-aware policy
execution, query-relative preservation, persistent model identity, separate statistical coverage,
and scoped correction remained programme obligations. The build-out's finite joint-law and
certificate questions became the next bounded target.

## Known gaps

The original substrate v1 bytes, its original 20-test review harness/results, and the v1.1 local
conformance harness were unavailable. Their reported outcomes remained source-reported rather than
recreated. The portable build-out replay shared provenance and arithmetic with the authored
material; it was neither independent verification nor a formal proof.

## Direction after this milestone

The next work was a focused adversarial review and constructive completion of the finite joint-law,
conditional-identification, and decision-certificate slice—not expansion into a general platform.

# Diagnostic history erasure: adversarial research review

8 September 2026. Author review, not external peer review. Reviewed starting head
`5ea19f214ec89536f47fe20cfa5a4d62222b2598`; all prior editions and evidence are retained.
Final source identity and observations are in `verification/diagnostic_erasure/results.json`.

The question was whether genuinely different diagnostic histories can be merged while
preserving the specified continuation and shared-consistency queries. The selected
outcome is an obstruction, not a weaker restatement of the public-copy theorem.

## What survives the audit

The new odds lemma characterizes shared consistency in the binary, one-disclosure
class for interior beliefs and strictly positive chance. Necessity follows from the
exact Bayes odds identity at EVERY term of an arbitrary completely mixed sequence.
The continuity argument is essential. Solving two exact linear equations with only
solution zero is useful independent arithmetic corroboration, but by itself would
not exclude sequences of approximate solutions converging to zero. The analytical
proof uses incompatible limiting RATIOS, avoiding that invalid inference.

The source uses a single explicit four-rate witness across two models. In the target,
the required ratios are 3/5 and 5/3. Each target model separately has a positive-rate
witness, but the target specified assessment has no common sequence. Replacing the
posterior by zero gives another shared-consistent assessment. The impossibility is
therefore neither failure of a bounded witness search nor nonexistence of equilibrium
in the underlying target game. It is exactly nonexistence for the specified query.

Every player moves once per path. Consequently all local pure continuations really
are the two available actions, and their values are checked individually. The source
and target conditional action values agree, not just their maxima. Broader global
strategies contingent on z are NOT covered. A criterion that required a single global
plan representing those strategies would already reject the reduction; the theorem
does not refute that stronger criterion. No unknown model oracle is used.

The receiver-only repair preserves the sender's four information sets and their
witness terms. It changes B's observation before B has acted, retaining perfect recall.
The new complete belief over four nodes is induced by the witness, with marginal
quality one half. The conditional values therefore agree. General source sequences
may require a common subsequence to obtain full-belief limits; finitely many models
and histories justify the compactness step. Neither reverse consistency preservation
nor transport to an arbitrary externally supplied full belief follows. The executable
case receives the particular four-history beliefs independently using QQ polynomials.

## Attacks and boundaries retained

- Equal current actions with a later diagnostic guess: remembered success 1 versus
  1/2 for every signal-blind mixture. An earlier plan needs the discarded information.
- Mechanism changes: bond 2 and extra defect gain 3/2 give gains -1/2 and +1/2 at
  detection probabilities 1 and 1/2; averaging monitoring masks the latter deviation.
- Equal values and zero gains with different likelihood restrictions: the main
  source/target pair satisfies the exact value criterion and still loses common
  consistency-existence. It is outside the old copy theorem because conditional
  quality laws differ across signals.
- Individually valid model reductions do not compose: two separately accepted target
  warrants require incompatible common ratios. This is the missing bridge resolved
  negatively, rather than another test of invalid witness restriction.
- Erased public coordination: replay the frozen matching counterexample rather than
  rewrite it. No general outcome-law claim is made here.
- Equal ratios do not prove impossibility: the new receiver rejects such a claimed
  obstruction. Interior beliefs, complete class structure, positive chance,
  marginalization, exact information maps and recipient-owned identity/query are
  explicit premises. Changed utilities or a broader game cannot borrow the odds
  conclusion merely by reusing its labels.

## Literature and implementation disposition

The foundation records primary-source inspection of MSI/USI definitions and sequential
results, CFR/well-formed and CRSWF bounds, power-sequence consistency, evidence games,
PBE sufficient-information representations and robust-game preferences. The recent
MSI/USI result is particularly relevant: its conditions range over strategy profiles,
not just the supplied assessment. It is not a common-family specified-belief transport
theorem. Our diagnostic erasure fails the reward-sufficiency condition under simple
alternative sender behavior, so no contradiction with that literature is claimed.

The inspected GTE solver handles general consistency constraints using matrix and
Wolfram machinery. This problem has an elementary exact characterization, so that
solver was not installed or executed. Existing rational continuation and SymPy QQ
receivers were reused, and SymPy linear algebra supplies an independent arithmetic
cross-check. No optimization, Rust or Lean escalation is justified. The elementary
proof is not formally verified; the main assurance comes from explicit quantifiers,
class recognition, negative controls and separately implemented positive receiving.

The receiving deployment excludes all candidate producers. It checks supplied
identities against separately passed recipient identities, all positive warrants,
every local action value and the exact obstruction premises. This is a bounded
reference, not a generic malformed-input/security framework or a quotient finder.
Ordinary and optimized runs must agree. Inherited acceptance compares frozen files
before detached replay; only living guidance and workflow entrypoints may change.

## Disposition

Retain as completion route B. The result materially advances disclosure/history
preservation: a diagnostic label can be irrelevant to current assessed incentives
while remaining indispensable to a common consistency sequence. A restricted useful
alternative is to retain the sender's likelihood-generating information while reducing
the receiver's decision memory with a witness-induced assessment. The mechanism
example establishes a composition boundary, not a new mechanism theorem.

Keep the next programme gate on explicit strategy-class deviation coverage and
likelihood sufficiency with common-model quantifiers. All-equilibria transport,
approximate consistency, empirical applicability and general quotient search remain
unearned. Do not call this an engineering adapter or causal result.

# Primary-source audit

Source identities are in [manifest.json](manifest.json). Dates identify inspected
versions; author-hosted drafts can change. These are targeted readings, not claims
to a complete literature review. No novelty claim follows from not finding a match.

## Parametric optimization

Feinberg, Kasyanov and Kraemer, *Continuity of Parametric Optima for Possibly
Discontinuous Functions and Noncompact Decision Sets*, arXiv:2109.06299v1 (2021),
[primary text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.06299v1).
Read introduction, §3.1 Definition 1/Theorem 1, and the lower-continuity discussion
in §3.2. Theorem 1 credits Tian–Zhou's feasible-path-transfer criterion: nearby
parameters must admit sufficiently good feasible choices. This is the existing
basis for our exact recovery condition. Theorem R is a compact specialization
with a direct proof; strict slack is only a convenient sufficient condition.
Theorem F is elementary finite-phase pasting, not a new maximum theorem.

## Discontinuous communication values

Lipnowski, Ravid and Shishkin, *Persuasion via Weak Institutions*, JPE 130(10)
(2022), DOI 10.1086/720462, [author PDF](https://elliotlipnowski.com/wp-content/uploads/PWI.pdf).
Read the model and §B–C, especially Propositions 2–3 and their main-text argument.
Proposition 2 already characterizes absence of trust collapse across credibility
levels and full-support priors; Proposition 3 studies robustness at commitment.
Thus communication discontinuities and their classification are established
subjects. Their optimized sender payoff and credibility technology differ from
our minimum enforcement fine under a supplied gate and hard evidence. The source
does not establish our exact thresholds, and we do not claim to subsume its theory.

## Belief-based signaling

Koessler, Laclau and Tomala, *A belief-based approach to signaling*, January 15,
2026, [author draft](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1tOPbqJrWtDDtZNMC5fBfp4k2UjL8kwIe/view).
Read §2–3, especially Theorem 1/INTIR, and §7.1–7.2 on verifiable evidence and
multiple receivers. The shared continuation-payoff vector that deters every
sender type is established belief-based signaling mathematics. Our pooling
inequalities are an explicit small-game specialization. Replacing that common
vector by independently selected typewise minima is invalid. The source does
not supply continuity of our parameterized admissible-law set.

## Informed communication

Koessler and Skreta, *Informed communication equilibrium*, June 2, 2026,
[author draft](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1LDie6EXqrksQYja4fM5eLVg2T7OGNcpn/view).
Read the common belief-tilt construction and Definition 6/Theorem 1, pp. 9–13.
Its interim individual-rationality condition uses one consistent belief/equilibrium
witness across types. Our finite public disclosure game uses related incentive
logic, but does not implement its private-player-information communication-device
model. Importing its terminology cannot justify arbitrary receiver-specific
posteriors in Bellman's game.

## Novelty decision

Existing mathematics suffices for the general continuity foundation. The useful
result here is the exact, assumption-audited specialization and its failure
examples. A paper could explain this boundary decomposition and compare gate
choice with a supplied gate, but publication novelty remains unestablished.
An unrestricted “only support or equilibrium changes matter” theorem is false:
the admissible-law catalogue counterexample identifies a missing information-set
regularity hypothesis. A claim based only on equilibrium nonemptiness also fails.

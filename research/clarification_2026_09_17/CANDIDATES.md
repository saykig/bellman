# Three candidate questions

Choose by contribution to cumulative, correctable understanding, not by novelty
or conformity with Bellman's current components.

| Candidate | Capability, objects and operations | Closest result | Pursue/drop criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Exact accumulation of partial descriptions | Relations over shared mechanism coordinates; join, projection and compatibility. Can two descriptions jointly entail something new? | Information algebras; AC extension criterion; BFMY join dependencies; credal separability boundaries | Baseline, not main next question: existing theory settles finite exact conjunction. Pursue only if new evidence semantics or a concrete scalability obstacle appears. Drop a new bridge claim. |
| 2. Query-preserving causal interfaces | Mechanism families; fixed intervention menu; kernel wiring and output restriction. What distinctions are needed for future causal uses? | LT query/component abstraction; Amato–Scozzari operation-relative completeness | Useful subproblem. Output-only continuation is settled by function/kernel composition. Internal access changes permissions. Do not pursue another large grid absent a precise new access/operation claim. |
| 3. Revision-aware accumulation | Labelled assumptions plus mechanism versions; addition, named withdrawal, replacement; exact intervention-answer sets and support status | ATMS environment labels; observational completeness of a specified transition system | Select. A current family provably cannot implement named withdrawal; one proof-support record can overinvalidate a conclusion with another surviving proof. Test a source-sensitive state and exactness across operation sequences. Drop novelty ambitions if the instantiation follows existing results cleanly. |

Candidate 3 incorporates 1 as its complete reference semantics and 2 as its query
and access discipline. It asks whether accumulated understanding can be corrected,
rather than making representation compression an end in itself.

Provisional Bellman assessment: explicit causal premises, exact compatible-model
families and shared-choice constraints are central reusable mathematics. Join and
query-signature probes are useful diagnostic baselines. Probability valuations
are useful for later stochastic calculations, but are not evidence-fusion licenses.
Decision certificates, games and strategic reduction are useful later, outside
this question. Current-answer-only summaries and anonymous intersected constraints
are potentially superseded *as revision states*, not invalidated as query results.
The seven-component architecture is neither adopted nor modified.

## Tempting overclaims rejected

1. The primary 90% Hoeffding interval is [-1,1]. Its breadth is a real information limitation,
   not a reason to choose a favorable different split. The four-session percentile bootstrap
   is descriptive sensitivity only. It cannot establish equivalence or substitute for a
   primary population-positive result. A narrower justified variance-sensitive method might
   be investigated later, but would be post-result methodological work, not preregistered evidence.
2. Deterministic hash splitting protects the implementation from outcome-based split selection;
   it does not prove exchangeability. The source study is published and its aggregate outcomes
   were inspected. The test is retrospective and only held out from this fitting procedure.
3. Ridge improves over persistence by a modest amount on this split. Stable tendencies,
   session context and learning remain confounded. Neither importance of a lagged feature nor
   conditional association identifies causal response to returns.
4. Rematching removes a stable relationship across rounds; history can reflect experiences
   with different people. It neither makes all observations independent nor prevents learning
   about the participant pool. The protocol/article's differing strength of the rematching
   description is preserved rather than silently resolved into guaranteed unique partners.
5. Assigned treatment contrasts concern availability of punishment/required justification.
   Actual punishment and actual explanations are endogenous. The session OLS interaction's
   wide interval cannot certify a positive synergy, and its t distribution relies on a
   session-error model. The unavailable allocation schedule precludes an exact assignment test.
6. Choosing an action does not identify utility or beliefs. Source theoretical equilibrium,
   empirical regression, ambiguity-sensitive preference and human authority remain separate.
7. The new intended use of hiding feedback is not the old prediction query. Portable revision
   receiving rejects it and keeps old evidence valid. No native Writ import occurred.

## Disposition

Retain the case as a reproducible, source-bound empirical research result with a useful
identification obstruction and a modest, uncertain predictive comparison. It improves Bellman's
handling of information-history queries but earns no general behavioral or credibility theory.
The dataset's predictive result may motivate a refined question about persistence versus
response to experience; that revision needs its own declared evaluation and honest exploratory
status. It is not a reason to discard the original observations.

The next proving-ground recommendation is the separate design checkpoint, which considers
continuing partners, elicited beliefs and changed incentives. It must not be mechanically
completed by enlarging this case's fixtures or treating repeated rounds as new independent units.
Current combined validation is supplied additively under verification/empirical_acceptance;
all v0.0.7 foundations, reviews, data evidence and former acceptance sources remain preserved.

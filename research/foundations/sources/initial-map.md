# Initial source map (2026-09-17)

Primary sources discovered; full-text checking and claim locators are in progress.

- Shenoy & Shafer (1990), [Axioms for Probability and Belief-Function Propagation](https://www.glennshafer.com/assets/downloads/articles/article43.pdf): combination, marginalization, local computation.
- Kohlas & Schmid, [Commutative Information Algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.15090): idempotent combination and extraction.
- Abramsky & Brandenburger (2011), [The Sheaf-Theoretic Structure of Non-Locality and Contextuality](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0264): compatible local distributions versus global extensions.
- Abramsky & Carù (2019), [Non-locality, contextuality and valuation algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.03521): an existing explicit connection; do not propose this bridge as new.
- Fong (2013), [Causal Theories](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.6201): typed stochastic composition; note errata on the abstract page.
- Pearl (2009), [Causal inference in statistics: An overview](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/6d71p32b): mechanism replacement and truncated factorization.
- Rischel & Weichwald (2021), [Compositional abstraction error and a category of causal models](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v161/rischel21a.html): composition of abstractions, which must be distinguished from wiring mechanisms.
- Lorenz & Tull (2026), [Causal and Compositional Abstraction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16612): recent related work; preliminary abstract-level screening only.

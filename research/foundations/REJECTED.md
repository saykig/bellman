# Rejected routes and retained boundaries

Dated 2026-09-17; these are research decisions, not failures erased from history.

| Proposal | Disposition and reason | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Invent a new valuation/sheaf bridge | Reject novelty premise: an explicit connection exists | AC Theorem 4.1 and Proposition 5.1 |
| Choose one universal algebra now | Defer: conjunction, factor multiplication and directed causal wiring have different meanings and laws | COMPARISON; DEVELOPMENT §1 |
| Multiply reported marginal distributions | Reject without a factorization/separator correction | B=(3/4,1/4) becomes (9/10,1/10) |
| Pairwise agreement proves a global extension | Reject on cyclic covers | X1 parity obstruction |
| A nonempty join preserves every local possibility | Reject | AB unrestricted, BC/AC equality example |
| Observational gluing supplies a causal model | Reject identification claim | X3 same observational law, different do law |
| Forget shared mechanism choices before composition | Reject exactness; retain only as a named outer relaxation | X2 coupled flips |
| Infer all input rows from observed rows | Reject without support or additional premises | X4 off-support intervention |
| A normalized marginal kernel accounts for shared noise | Reject | X5 correlated fair outputs |
| Hide an internal node, then intervene on it | Reject unless intervention-indexed semantics or mechanism structure is retained | T3 scope |
| General feedback via multiplication/normalization | Reject in this investigation; may have zero or multiple solutions | T3 cyclic examples |
| Rebuild the current certificate/strategic infrastructure | Out of scope; preserve its artifacts | Repository inspection and user brief |
| Formalize a universal causal category immediately | Reject cost and unstable semantics; test the narrow relational theorem first | Lean evaluation records its precise scope |

Open alternatives, not disproved: credal networks, shared-latent SCMs, and richer
causal abstractions may be appropriate for later cases. The present recommendation
is conditional on the selected questions and does not establish their inferiority.

# Adversarial review and disposition

2026-09-09. Author review, not external peer review or formal verification.
Scope: SPEC.md at cca3103, initial cases/reference at 1c35622, and additive checks.

1. **Persistent model:** expanding p(s)K(x|s)L(a(x),s) keeps the same s throughout.
   A state-indexed action is inadmissible. The checker enumerates outcome-only
   functions, not separate policies for each state. Hidden policy fields reject.
2. **Quantifier:** a supplied point prior weights utility/type hypotheses. Nothing
   establishes simultaneous optimality for every state or every prior. Perfect
   type measurement resolves statewise disagreement in this case, but the optimal
   paid Bayesian measurement need not. This distinction survives the report.
3. **Net comparison:** each donor's acquisition minimum would hide the cost of a
   rejected test. The wrapper uses forced observation risk and an explicit free
   baseline. Costs, entire menu and common loss/query identity are bound.
4. **Zero value:** equality in the nonnegative branch-regret sum proves the iff
   criterion, including zero-probability outcomes by excluding them. The .51
   report is an exact falsification of information change implying decision value.
5. **Utility hypothesis:** unknown utility represented as hidden state requires
   cardinal comparability and an evaluator's prior. Normalizing at 39 and 51
   specifies that convention; it does not derive it. A perfect utility report is
   a stipulated design option, not an available empirical instrument. A costly
   cash elicitation under unknown curvature cannot silently use this scalar fee.
6. **Behavior/commitment:** a hypothetical conditional report and an actual
   deviation are different experiments. State-changing probes, report incentives
   and sequential plan credibility are outside the static engine. The two plans
   are supplied commitment actions. No new equilibrium theorem is earned.
7. **Reuse:** engine source is pinned and copied to an ephemeral source-only
   directory. Its checkout is untouched. Full receiving blocks solver imports,
   also tests that the import actually fails, and rechecks donor answers. The
   public checker independently enumerates policies but checks only the claimed
   risk/action/ranking operation, not all donor metadata. The modes are explicit.
8. **Falsification:** 15 negative controls reject invalid channels/prior/cost,
   missing baseline/result, hidden-model or transition fields, stale subject,
   false ranking/risk/action, omitted cost, relabelled signal and changed costs
   or losses. A zero-prior/impossible-outcome fixture preserves action and
   acquisition ties. These are finite executable evidence, not a completeness
   theorem about every malformed Python object or formal assurance of a parser.
9. **Practicality:** one JSON interface solves both designed cases without
   engine edits. The private donor is a real portability limitation: full
   receiving requires authorized existing access. The public direct audit works
   without it. No licence is invented and no private source is redistributed.

Disposition: the bounded Bayesian menu theorem and examples survive this review.
The useful earned operation is ranking possible measurements for a specified
loss/prior decision, including rejecting information that is not worth its cost.
This advances measurement design and query-specific information preservation; it
does not materially advance strategic equilibrium or general mechanism composition.
The stopping condition is this exact conditional operation plus preserved inherited
acceptance. Empirical acquisition/utility calibration and robust menu optimization
remain separate future questions, not implicit completion obligations.

# Rejected shortcuts and remaining questions

1. **Fresh independent extrema in each state under a shared budget.** The
   endpoint face can cost more than κ. Theorems A/B and exact counterexample 1
   repair the representation using minimum-cost profiles and one common budget.
2. **Add separate worst expected gains.** Counterexample 2 proves strict excess.
   Theorem E instead maximizes six common-law affine branches. Conversely, retain
   separate universal inequalities when testing exact zero gain: that conjunction
   is valid even when their maximizers differ.
3. **Sum KL divergences of the originally specified local marginals.** They can
   all vanish while the full law has positive dependence cost. Augmented event
   tables and separator corrections recover the needed minimum cost under D.
4. **Treat a gate's public output as voluntary disclosure.** It supplies no actor
   who knows extra information. F explicitly adds a privately informed advocate,
   verifiable evidence, timing, payoff and continuation game.
5. **Assume silence stays uninformative after allowing disclosure.** Only the
   pooling candidate has the old posterior; any other strategy requires Bayes
   updating. F's impossibility uses a forced certificate continuation and cannot
   be evaded by arbitrary silent beliefs.
6. **Use an independently worst punishment for each sender type.** The three-type
   example requires one q after the same partial certificate; its incompatible
   individual minima falsely certify pooling. G keeps the joint continuation set.
7. **Automatically identify this with Saas's disclosure-proof CE theorem.** His
   payoff-irrelevant signals and punishment construction do not establish our
   advocate's verified-state incentive. Certification literature is a closer
   general foundation. No novelty claim follows from different terminology.
8. **Generalize binary symmetry by assumption.** A–D and G state finite, asymmetric
   versions with explicit limits. The six-branch count and b−c/η threshold remain
   specific to the benchmark; arbitrary games do not inherit them.
9. **Claim optimizer agreement or old Lean logs prove the new theorems.** Written
   proofs supply the claims; new computations test them. Older optimizer failures
   and formalization boundaries remain in the supplied audit unchanged.
10. **Start a universal framework or architecture migration.** Existing convex
    analysis, probability compatibility and certification games suffice to host
    these results. Next work should solve the stated partial-certificate frontier.

Unresolved: efficient joint transcript profiles, non-Markov references, endogenous
information costs, multiple informed senders/private receiver signals, strong
consistency with such signals, and a general convex representation of feasible
continuation payoff vectors. Historical novelty and full Forges–Koessler subsumption
remain unverified. No claim of general supermodularity/submodularity, equilibrium
uniqueness, empirical incentives, welfare optimality or credible enforcement is made.

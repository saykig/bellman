# Primary sources and the exact comparison

Inspected 18 September 2026. This is targeted prior-work positioning, not proof
of historical novelty. Supplied model/audit identities are in INPUT_MANIFEST.json.
No earlier numerical or Lean result is relabelled as new evidence.

| Source and inspected location | What is borrowed | What it does not establish for this run |
|---|---|---|
| Dobra–Fienberg (2000), [author PDF](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/adobra/Files/Papers/pnas.pdf), Theorem 6, Eq.(5), pp.11887–11888 | Sharp cell bounds for decomposable fixed-marginal systems. Normalize counts to probabilities. | Sharp individual cells do not imply simultaneous attainment under an added shared entropy budget. No new name is assigned to their probability formula. |
| Wainwright–Jordan, [author-hosted manuscript](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~jordan/sail/readings/wainwright-jordan-fnt.pdf), §2.5.2, Eq.(2.10), Proposition 1 | Clique/separator factorization and equivalence of local and global marginal consistency on junction trees. | Our flag-augmented KL calculation is a specialization of this machinery, not a new gluing theorem. The paper's exact-inference premise must not be mistaken for independence of every completion of fixed marginals. |
| Csiszár–Shields (2004), [author tutorial](https://www.renyi.hu/~csiszar/Publications/Information_Theory_and_Statistics:_A_Tutorial.pdf), Theorem 3.2, Remark 3.1 | I-projection Pythagorean identity on linear families; support-aware treatment of exponential families. | The profile construction uses established partial minimization. General projection theory alone does not select the strategic query or solve the six active-incentive branches. |
| Boyd–Vandenberghe, [Convex Optimization](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/), book landing page; standard duality chapter cited, PDF retrieval timed out | Lagrange weak/strong duality and strict feasibility conditions. | No scalability guarantee or exact floating-point certificate follows. The direct inequalities and attaining witnesses in our proofs supply the finite specialization. |
| Shapiro (2016), [author working paper](https://optimization-online.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/4613.pdf), §2, Theorems 2.2–2.3 | Rectangularity, support-function comparisons and preservation for a specified objective class. | Our coordinate endpoint-face criterion is elementary convex geometry; it is not a new general rectangularity theorem or a dynamic time-consistency result. |
| Bergemann–Brooks–Morris (2022), [author PDF](https://benjaminbrooks.net/downloads/bbm_counterfactuals.pdf), §3.1, Theorem 1 | Component outcomes share an information structure iff they are marginals of a linked-game BCE. | This is an existential outcome characterization. It does not by itself solve our uniform-penalty robust frontier over a fixed KL-restricted family and fixed gate. |
| Galperti–Perego, [author draft](https://www.jperego.com/files/papers/GP/draft.pdf), §3.3, Theorem 2; published Theoretical Economics 20 (2025), 667–711 | Spillover-robust obedience under specified network and seed restrictions, using mixed-action recommendations in the general formulation. | Source-dependence KL budgets are additional restrictions. We are not claiming network restrictions, their general revelation result, or information spillovers are new. |
| Milgrom–Roberts (1986), [author scan](https://web.stanford.edu/~milgrom/publishedarticles/Relying%20on%20the%20Information%20of%20Interested%20Parties.pdf), abstract p.18 and discussion p.20 visually inspected | Voluntary verifiable evidence, skeptical inference and the importance of known preferences and knowledge about withheld information. | Their complete-information decision conclusions depend on substantive preference/evidence assumptions. We derive our advocate threshold from explicit continuations rather than assert universal unraveling. |
| Saas (2026), [author manuscript](https://eller.arizona.edu/sites/default/files/2025-10/Disclosure_Proof_Correlated_Equilibria-8.pdf), §3, Theorem 1, Corollary 1; [publication](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geb.2026.08.001) | Disclosure-proof CE characterized by obedience and interim payoff floors from feasible punishments; two-player floors use worst Nash payoffs. | Signals there carry no payoff-relevant information. Our informed third-party advocate verifies a payoff state; the fixed penalty and partial-certificate game need their own continuation checks. F and G are not applications obtained by silently replacing his type space. |
| Forges–Koessler (2005), [publisher description](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304406804000874), abstract and introduction's description of Theorems 3.1–3.2 | General certification-equilibrium framework with type-dependent available reports and canonical representations. | This is closer than ordinary persuasion to G. The full PDF was inaccessible (403); a theorem-by-theorem subsumption claim remains unverified. Our G is proved directly and is not claimed as a new characterization of all certification outcomes. |
| Hagenbach–Koessler–Perez-Richet (2014), [author paper](https://edurichet.github.io/papers/HardInfoV13.pdf), abstract; [online appendix](https://edurichet.github.io/papers/onlineappendix.pdf), opening equilibrium discussion | Full revelation in certifiable pre-play communication; distinction between strong consistency and weaker evidence-consistent off-path beliefs. | Our pooling question is different. One sender with only public receiver information permits an explicit common tremble construction; multiple privately informed parties cannot inherit it without checking consistency. |
| Kamenica–Gentzkow (2011), [author PDF](https://web.stanford.edu/~gentzkow/research/BayesianPersuasion.pdf), Proposition 1 and §II.C | Bayes-plausible posterior splitting under commitment, and the role of commitment. | A privately informed sender deciding whether to release existing evidence is not automatically allowed to commit to an optimal experiment. The gate is committed; the advocate is not. |
| Denti (2023), [published paper](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.3982/TE4541), §2.1, Definition 2 | Equilibrium information acquisition must maximize its benefit net of cost; players may want to learn others' information. | Our disclosure actor starts informed. There is no acquisition choice or endogenously justified KL budget. k is a disclosure cost, κ an analyst restriction; they are different objects. |

Source limits: Milgrom's PDF is scanned and had no extracted text, so selected
pages were rendered and inspected. Publisher text and author manuscripts were
used rather than treating search summaries as proof of mathematical details.
The 1975 Csiszár landing page was unavailable; the inspected coauthored tutorial
supplies the precise projection result used here. Wainwright–Jordan's linked PDF
has a draft header, so locators refer to that manuscript, not inferred published
pagination. The supplied audit's expectile and rare-revelation results remain
borrowed; neither is needed to establish the new six-branch formula.

**Remaining contribution gap:** none of the inspected statements directly gives
the six-branch active benchmark or its joint controlled/disclosure penalty. General
convex analysis and certification theory are broad enough to host them. The earned
contribution is an explicit exact solution and sharp failures of two plausible
independent-summary rules. Priority, general efficiency, and a broader disclosure
frontier with shared partial certificates remain unestablished.

# Failed approaches and overclaims to reject

1. **Perturb public information inside the predecessor at a fixed fine.** With
   fully informed sender, state-only utilities and full eligible support, the
   entire posterior simplex remains available. F1 explains why the desired
   continuation switch cannot be manufactured this way. No randomized search
   over payoff tables was performed or needed for this obstruction.
2. **Reuse the predecessor's payoff-delta example as information-only.** Rejected:
   its receiver utilities vary. The new example fixes utilities, costs AND prior.
3. **Treat the coarse sender as fully informed by implication.** Rejected; user
   authorized both models. Changing the information partition is explicit.
4. **Use weak PBE to pin unused-certificate beliefs.** Rejected: Bayes on path
   alone does not do this. Sequential consistency is essential. Under arbitrary
   off-path beliefs the same game admits zero fine for all information parameters.
5. **Claim to kill strong persistence by making its witness disappear.** Rejected:
   C3 violates near-optimal persistence. It refutes only the weak existence reading.
6. **Infer smoothing from an arbitrarily noisy informative gate.** Rejected: every
   positive contrast still breaks the receiver tie. If zero contrast is excluded
   but approachable, the infimum can jump and need not be attained.
7. **Call three states/actions globally minimal.** Unproved. This is a small
   witness; other game forms or incentive mechanisms may need fewer actions.
8. **Require every optimizer or equilibrium to persist.** Too strong for value
   continuity. R2 permits replacement gates and witnesses, uniformly over laws.
9. **Make a large numerical frontier or introduce Lean/Julia/Rust.** No trigger:
   elementary exact inequalities settle the continuum; rational Python checks
   the finite evidence. No formal proof or production checker is claimed.

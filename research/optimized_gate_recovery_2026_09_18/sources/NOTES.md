## Parametric optimization: the general theorem is established

Feinberg, Kasyanov and Kraemer, *Continuity of Parametric Optima for Possibly
Discontinuous Functions and Noncompact Decision Sets*,
[arXiv:2109.06299v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2109.06299v1), especially §3.1,
Definition 1/Theorem 1 and §3.2–3.3. Their feasible-path-transfer criterion,
credited to Tian–Zhou, supplies the existing basis for R2. R1 is the compact
closed-graph argument and R3 is a finite-cover sufficient condition. Jointly
optimizing the gate merely enlarges the decision variable; it does not replace
recovery assumptions. F2 derives those assumptions from a restricted game.
No new general continuity result is claimed.

## Signaling: beliefs and common incentive witnesses are established

Koessler, Laclau and Tomala, *A belief-based approach to signaling*,
[July 8, 2025 primary draft](https://www.cerge-ei.cz/pdf/events/papers/3.%20bbs.pdf),
§3, pp. 6–8, INTIR and Theorem 1. Their interim condition uses a common belief and
receiver response to control every sender type. This is the relevant baseline
for F1/F2; independent typewise punishment choices are invalid. In our coarse
sender example, payoff states 1 and 2 belong to ONE sender information type, so
relative state beliefs within that type cannot be independently tilted. One must
preserve the state-to-type map, not identify types with payoff states by notation.
The fine/gate formula here is a specialization, not a replacement of their theory.

The [author's current page](https://sites.google.com/site/frederickoessler/Research)
links a January 2026 draft. Its Drive viewer did not expose the text to this run.
The July 2025 version was read directly instead. Do not claim to have reverified
all sections of the January 2026 draft cited in the preceding Bellman study.

## Communication discontinuities: the subject is already developed

Lipnowski, Ravid and Shishkin, *Persuasion via Weak Institutions*, JPE 130(10),
2022, DOI 10.1086/720462,
[author PDF](https://elliotlipnowski.com/wp-content/uploads/PWI.pdf), main-text
Propositions 2–3, PDF pp. 21–22. They study discontinuous communication values
and robustness as institutional credibility changes, including full-support
priors. Our objective is instead minimum receiver fine under source uncertainty,
a gate constraint and hard evidence. Their results do not establish (C3), and
(C3) does not supersede their classification. Full-support discontinuities are
not themselves a novelty claim.

## What remains new, if anything?

No novel general mechanism or theorem has been established. The retained
contribution is an explicit rational diagnostic example and an audited boundary:
full state knowledge versus eligibility knowledge, off-path consistency versus
arbitrary beliefs, and gate availability versus uniform near-optimal recovery.
No exact match for the displayed payoff table was established in this targeted
reading; that is not evidence of publication novelty. Established mathematics
settles the bounded general continuity question under the stated premises.

# Rejected approaches and reasons

1. **Claim a new unifying theory before solving a game.** The three-state frontier
   was solved first. The source audit then found close existing upper-set and
   interim-payoff theory. Use those foundations and make a smaller contribution.
2. **Independent worst-case source intervals.** Endpoints may spend the same
   shared budget multiple times. Retain likelihood cost profiles under one budget.
3. **A separate credible punishment for each hidden type.** The certificate is
   common; the continuation must be common. At e<.8,k=0 the example needs both
   q≤.2 and q≥.8. Individual minima conceal infeasibility.
4. **Treat KL as essential to the profile theorem.** Its proof uses attained local
   minima and independent conditional selection. KL-specific dual/tree formulas
   are extra structure, as the exact χ² counterexample demonstrates.
5. **Close or convexify every feasible set.** Unattained zero cost and the
   nonconvex credible-payoff example give false positives at threshold boundaries.
6. **Use a limiting full-support posterior at a perfect gate.** The eligible type
   can become impossible. The exact fine frontier jumps; beliefs must respect support.
7. **Import arbitrary-channel optimality from the binary game.** The three-state
   policy task differs. This run solves and optimizes the stipulated symmetric
   binary gate class, and makes no claim over all signal mechanisms.
8. **Treat all modelwise equilibria as one unknown-model implementable policy.**
   Players here know the actual law. Changing that premise changes the quantifiers.
9. **Use SLSQP for a nonsmooth TV check.** It returned unsuccessful status and a
   material objective gap. An exact LP formulation replaces it; failed numerical
   output is not theoretical evidence.
10. **Generalize the one-sender belief choice freely to multiple senders.**
    Independent private sender trembles impose a rank-one likelihood restriction.
    Locally admissible posterior marginals do not establish global consistency.
11. **Make a priority claim from a failed PDF retrieval.** Close 2026 primary
    sources already cover the central joint-payoff logic. The remaining 2005
    access gap is recorded, not exploited.

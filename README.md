# Bellman

## Programme north star

> Make consequential decision-making mathematically inspectable, cumulative, and correctable.

The ambition may remain enormous while the first demonstrated competence is narrow and exact.

Bellman is the **mathematical research programme for consequential decision-making**. It assembles and develops mathematical objects, assumptions, operations, guarantees, and composition rules that make decision knowledge inspectable, cumulative, and correctable. Established mathematics is welcome; novelty is not a condition for progress. Kahneman Lab's bounded experiments and theory closures remain part of that cumulative history.

The first capability to earn is to know exactly what information a representation must preserve for specified future decisions. The [North Star](foundations/BELLMAN_NORTH_STAR.md) retains the programme's full direction and component obligations.

## Current programme steering

The [Programme Roadmap](docs/programme/ROADMAP.md) is the living capability map: what is hardened, what is next, what remains open, what is closed, and when the programme should redirect rather than defend a stale plan.

The [Bellman–Writ Mathematical Transfer Architecture](docs/programme/ARCHITECTURE.md) records how mathematical results move from Bellman into bounded computation, checking, Writ provenance/revision infrastructure, and eventually domain stress tests. These are living steering documents; they do not supersede frozen mathematical artifacts or evidence.

The [sequential credibility component](foundations/BELLMAN_SEQUENTIAL_CREDIBILITY_UNDER_UNCERTAINTY.md) combines model uncertainty, private evidence, remembered information, a bond and monitoring in one bounded game. Its preliminary assessment-relative result is preserved. The [reviewed consistency addendum](foundations/BELLMAN_SEQUENTIAL_CREDIBILITY_CONSISTENCY_ADDENDUM.md) and [current reproduction guide](verification/diagnostic_erasure/ACCEPTANCE.md) check one shared power-perturbation witness and full continuation incentives: at zero tolerance, a pass establishes sequential equilibrium separately in every supplied model with a common consistency witness. This is not an ambiguity-preference equilibrium, empirical prediction, general history reduction or complete equilibrium search. The research remains separate from the causal build.

The [public-tag history reduction](foundations/BELLMAN_PUBLIC_TAG_HISTORY_REDUCTION.md) proves a specified many-to-one reduction preserving both continuation incentives and common off-path consistency. It erases an initial public tag only when each tag branch is an identical continuation game with the same supplied strategy and assessment. Public coordination and altered off-path likelihoods give explicit counterexamples to broader erasure claims. This remains a conditional research result under review.

The [diagnostic erasure obstruction](foundations/BELLMAN_DIAGNOSTIC_HISTORY_ERASURE_OBSTRUCTION.md) goes beyond identical copies: erasing genuinely different diagnostic histories can preserve every local continuation value yet destroy shared consistency-existence. The proof covers arbitrary tremble sequences, even though each reduced model works separately. Retaining the sender’s diagnostic information permits a checked receiver-only memory reduction with a witness-induced assessment. This is a bounded obstruction and repair, not general history compression.

The [build-out review](reviews/BELLMAN_BUILDOUT_ADVERSARIAL_REVIEW.md) and original build-out remain unchanged historical inputs. The [research history](docs/history/README.md) indexes the substrate sequence, experiments, repairs, source gaps, and completed research milestones. The [PR4 reference repair](verification/pr4_repair/README.md) records exact-witness, immutable-subject, and complete-task validation fixes. It separates current code/check evidence from the unchanged historical mathematical record.

The [sequential certificate construction](foundations/BELLMAN_SEQUENTIAL_CERTIFICATE_COMPOSITION.md) connects checked local inequalities to whole-policy regret, conditioning, and replanning, with [fixed exact checks](verification/sequential_certificates/README.md). Its bounded history-tree reference extends the mathematics without defining the programme's ceiling.

The [certificate transport construction](foundations/BELLMAN_CERTIFICATE_TRANSPORT_AND_REVALIDATION.md) revalidates sequential guarantees after explicit model or policy changes. It proves anchored lower/upper envelopes, binds positive-prefix conditioning to the target model, and supplies [fixed exact checks](verification/certificate_transport/README.md).

The [certificate accumulation construction](foundations/BELLMAN_CERTIFICATE_ACCUMULATION_AND_POLICY_SELECTION.md) combines same-policy bounds and separately constructs accessible policy selectors, with explicit provenance and exact-value conditions for actual dominance. See [verification](verification/certificate_accumulation/README.md).

The [persistent model-family construction](foundations/BELLMAN_PERSISTENT_MODEL_FAMILY_CERTIFICATES.md) checks one implementable history policy across alternative whole-episode models, separately verifies exact deterministic minimax-loss and minimax-regret choices, and filters conditional families by positive prefix support without model averaging or hidden-model switching. See [verification](verification/persistent_model_families/README.md).

The [family-replanning construction](foundations/BELLMAN_FAMILY_REPLANNING_AND_ROOT_GUARANTEES.md) constructs and checks one complete continuation splice in every fixed family member, distinguishes submitted-certificate cap preservation from exact policy change, and retains zero-mass members in ex-ante root guarantees. See [verification](verification/family_replanning/README.md).

The [anytime-valid Bernoulli data-to-decision bridge](foundations/BELLMAN_ANYTIME_VALID_DATA_TO_DECISION.md) constructs an outward-checked rational interval from a complete binary prefix under an explicit IID fixed-parameter premise, then separately checks exact static action-risk and named-action regret over that interval. Its simultaneous coverage survives repeated inspection and data-dependent stopping under the stated sampling assumptions. [Acceptance hardening](verification/statistical_decision_bridge/ACCEPTANCE_HARDENING.md) applies the declared finite support profile to producer and receiver routes and separates live checks from pinned historical replay without changing the mathematical companion. This remains neither a posterior, empirical validation, sequential-control theorem, nor Writ/Decision Lab integration. See [verification](verification/statistical_decision_bridge/README.md).

The [controlled multistream construction](foundations/BELLMAN_MULTISTREAM_COLLECTION_AND_DECISIONS.md) composes those scalar all-prefix guarantees across a fixed finite stream registry while a built-in history-only collector changes the next stream and stopping time. A separate receiver replays the complete transcript, checks rowwise scalar evidence and fixed alpha allocation, and verifies exact affine static decisions on the retained parameter rectangle. Cross-stream independence is not needed for the coverage union bound; concealed outcome filtering, conditional future-draw claims without a fresh-draw premise, and joint-event queries unsupported by marginals remain outside the result. Its [acceptance hardening](verification/multistream_collection/ACCEPTANCE_HARDENING.md) repairs public allocation/weight subject binding and revision-lineage classification while preserving the companion and original result evidence. See [verification](verification/multistream_collection/README.md).

The [statistical rectangle to corner-model construction](foundations/BELLMAN_STATISTICAL_RECTANGLES_TO_CORNER_MODEL_GUARANTEES.md) composes a checked multistream rectangle with persistent whole-episode sequential guarantees. When each uncertain parameter occurs in at most one transition factor on every complete skeleton path, every deterministic policy cost is multi-affine and its worst cost and modelwise regret occur exactly at rectangle corners. The bounded [receiver and exact checks](verification/statistical_corner_models/README.md) support at most two parameters/four corners, explicitly bind stream and transition identities, and reject the `p(1-p)` repeated-parameter failure without calling the underlying sequential problem impossible. Statistical premises remain supplied rather than empirically validated; corners are not assigned probabilities, and no randomized-policy or engineering-transfer claim is made.

The [unsafe-set reachability and constrained-selection construction](foundations/BELLMAN_UNSAFE_SET_REACHABILITY_AND_CONSTRAINED_SELECTION.md) computes the exact probability that one complete deterministic policy ever enters an explicitly identified unsafe observable history. Its bounded [receiver and exact checks](verification/unsafe_set_reachability/README.md) independently compare an absorbing recurrence with first-hit paths, retain unweighted persistent-model identities, and select deterministic policies within one fixed robust-feasible class. Under the inherited pathwise multi-affine warrant, rectangle-wide reachability is captured exactly by the statistical corner family. Expected loss, unsafe-hit probability, coverage failure, normative adequacy, and authority remain separate; no CVaR, dynamic-risk, randomized-policy, or engineering-transfer claim is made.

The [finite causal identification and decision construction](foundations/BELLMAN_FINITE_CAUSAL_IDENTIFICATION_AND_DECISION_CERTIFICATION.md) separates observational association from intervention. One exact route checks finite adjustment under explicitly supplied consistency, exchangeability, pre-action, adjustment-set, and positivity premises. A second constructs the exact eight-response-type binary causal fibre, distinguishes point/partial identification, incompatibility, unsupported nonlinear restrictions, and labeled outer relaxations, and checks action differences on the same causal witness. The bounded [receiver and exact checks](verification/finite_causal_identification/README.md) preserve the Simpson reversal, positivity failure, decision identification despite causal uncertainty, and opposite model-dependent decisions. This is conditional one-stage mathematics—not graph discovery, empirical validation, causal transport, sequential-treatment composition, authority, or Writ/Decision Lab integration.

The [two-stage longitudinal causal policy and sequential bridge](foundations/BELLMAN_TWO_STAGE_LONGITUDINAL_CAUSAL_POLICY_AND_SEQUENTIAL_BRIDGE.md) identifies a named adapted deterministic policy by the exact longitudinal g-formula under explicitly supplied consistency, sequential-exchangeability, adaptation, no-interference, and policy-specific positivity premises. Its bounded [receiver and exact checks](verification/longitudinal_causal_policy/README.md) require stronger all-action/all-history support before constructing Bellman's completed two-stage subject, then compare all 32 complete policies through the existing sequential certificate and independent path evaluators. Two fully supported worlds with identical one-stage `do(A2)` marginals but opposite optimal history-dependent policies prove that the prior one-stage intervention laws cannot be substituted for longitudinal transition kernels. This remains conditional mathematics under review—not premise validation, longitudinal partial identification, transport, safety/model-family composition, authority, or Writ/Decision Lab integration.

## Bellman, Writ, and executable mathematics

Writ is the engineering and provenance infrastructure intended to progressively embody stable Bellman mathematics. Bellman and Writ may remain separately implemented while their interfaces are developed. Repository separation is not permanent doctrine. Mature Bellman mathematics is expected to become executable engineering capability where justified, and the mathematics may continue beyond what present engineering implements.

[`saykig/writ-decision-lab`](https://github.com/saykig/writ-decision-lab) is the bounded computational test bench for the first executable Bellman slices. Build 1 (`finite-one-observation.v1`) and Build 2 (`finite-linear-uncertainty.v1`) are now merged there. Build 2 supplies a reusable producer/checker/consumer boundary for exact compatibility, linear-query extrema, and decision identification within its stated finite profile; the equal-assurance direct workflow remains a simpler comparison route. Decision Lab does not define Bellman's mathematical ceiling or the permanent Writ architecture.

## Record discipline

- Preregistrations, sealed inputs, lane reports, referee reports, and syntheses are frozen artifacts.
- Frozen artifacts are never edited in place. A correction or reinterpretation is added as a new, explicitly labelled artifact.
- Preregistered findings, post-hoc findings, replications, failures, conjectures, and theory closures remain distinct.
- Exact source bytes and SHA-256 digests are retained where available. Unavailable originals are recorded as gaps, not reconstructed and relabeled.
- Canonical documents and compact verification evidence belong here; ZIP bundles, local caches, temporary work, and large disposable logs do not.

## Writ transfer boundary

Nothing in this repository by itself authorizes a Writ architecture change.

A transfer requires:

1. a sufficiently established result;
2. a concrete Writ workflow that actually needs it; and
3. evidence that the smallest necessary requirement improves that workflow.

The eventual proving arena may include war, security strategy, and global affairs. That long-term application does not license premature formalization of political meaning, legitimacy, authority, ideology, or human judgment.

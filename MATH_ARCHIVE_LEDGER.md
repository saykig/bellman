# Mathematical Archive Ledger

This file preserves mathematical directions that were useful to the research process but are no longer active claims or default roadmaps. Archival status does **not** mean the underlying mathematics is false. It means the corresponding hypothesis, architecture claim, or experimental branch has been superseded, bounded, or closed by later evidence.

The active cumulative record remains [`FINDINGS_LEDGER.md`](FINDINGS_LEDGER.md).

## KL2 — retired architecture claims

### Retired: D0 as a sufficient general composition discipline

Loose pluralism can permit invalid reuse when shared dependence, query semantics, actor-time scope, authority, procedure, or implementation are lost. This remains a useful negative lesson, but D0 is no longer an active architecture candidate.

### Retired: D1 as a demonstrated architecture winner

The final KL2 adjudication retained D1 only as a narrow set of correctness obligations on the finite suite. Competent conventional analysis solved the complete cases, and the benchmark did not establish an exclusive D1 identification, theorem, or tractability advantage.

Do not cite KL2 as proving that Writ needs a D1 layer.

### Retired: a new decision-provenance mathematical object is presently necessary

The tested distinctions were expressible with existing relations, sets, stochastic kernels, structural causal models, preferences, optimization, games, and ordinary query-relative sufficiency arguments. No necessity burden for a new object was met.

### Archived but not disproved: persistent transport/recombination

Distributed reuse under information loss remains a legitimate possible wedge. KL2 did not test it directly enough to establish a persistent-layer advantage. It is archived as a candidate research direction, not a default Experiment 3.

## KL3v2 — retired preparation methodology

### Retired: D as a distinct preparation method

`KL3V2_STOP_CONVENTIONAL_SUFFICES` killed the claim that the specially instructed D preparation method had a distinctive advantage on the tested wedge. Conventional analysis matched or exceeded it under the preregistered Phase 1 conditions.

The useful survivor is not the D method; it is the generic concept of query-relative decision sufficiency.

## KL4 — archived false shortcuts

The following shortcuts should remain dead unless a later theorem explicitly restores them under narrower assumptions:

- entropy or mutual information as a proxy for decision value;
- probability-set width as a proxy for decision stability;
- one query-independent notion of a minimal sufficient record;
- nonpositive one-step information value as a general reason to stop observing.

KL4 itself remains a valid frozen experiment. These archived items are the hypotheses it defeated, not results to delete.

## KL5 — closed sequential-certificate branch

### Retired: `C_*` as a generally sufficient sequential certificate

KL5 Stage R already found equal-`C_*` models with different root sequential decisions in the primary finite domain. KL6 later supplied a strictly interior analytic separation. `C_*` therefore must not be revived as a generally sufficient representation for bounded sequential decisions.

### Preserved but non-retroactive: finer-grid post-hoc observation

The later eighth-grid observation suggested the quarter-grid interior failure was design-sensitive. It never changed the frozen `KL5_STOP_DEGENERATE` verdict and must not be rewritten as a preregistered KL5 success.

## KL6 — closed observation-model compression branch

### Retired: S04 as evidence of genuine compression

On the registered informative eighth-grid at horizon 5,

`S04 = (C_*, Z_5)`

identifies the labeled observation kernel itself. Its sufficiency is therefore reconstruction, not compression.

### Retired: rescue by another grid or expanded KL6 search

The KL6 closure explicitly forbids reopening the original question by adding another grid, alternate price, loss family, horizon, smoother kernel, representation ladder, parallel lane, or holdout after learning why S04 succeeds. Such work would be a new question, not a repair of KL6.

### Retired: KL7 as an automatic continuation of KL6

`KL6_STOP_THEORY_ALREADY_SETTLES` and `KL6_STOP_STANDARD` close this branch. No KL7 follows merely because a broader representation problem can be imagined.

## Post-KL6 frontier audit — retired frontier claim

### Retired: bounded-error guarantees for lossy decision representations are an unresolved frontier

The post-KL6 audit ended with `FRONTIER_STOP_EXISTING_THEORY_SUFFICES`.

Existing mathematical theory already contains exact and approximate abstraction, simulation, information-state, robust dynamic-programming, and value-directed compression results capable of providing bounded decision-error or policy-regret guarantees under explicit assumptions. The KL6 closure also contains a finite-horizon coupling argument with bounded optimal-value error, bounded surrogate-policy regret, and exact STOP/OBSERVE preservation outside a certified margin interval.

Therefore the programme should not spend another experiment proving merely that such guarantees can exist.

### Still available as a bounded research question

After a concrete query family and error criterion are fixed, it may still be scientifically useful to ask:

> What is the smallest, cheapest to maintain, or efficiently discoverable representation that preserves the required decisions or error guarantee?

This is an optimization/discovery question over representations, not a foundational claim that preservation mathematics is missing. It should compete with other decision-proving-ground directions rather than control the roadmap.

## Archive rule for future work

Move a mathematical direction here when later work shows that it is:

- falsified on its claimed scope;
- subsumed by established mathematics;
- no longer discriminating against the strongest null;
- superseded by a sharper question;
- or scientifically complete enough that another round would mainly protect the old roadmap.

Do not delete the historical record. Do not treat an archived hypothesis as active Writ architecture. Do not prevent it from returning if genuinely new evidence changes its status.

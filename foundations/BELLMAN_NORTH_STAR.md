# Bellman North Star

## Programme

> **Make consequential decision-making mathematically inspectable, cumulative, and correctable.**

## First capability to earn

> **Know exactly what information a representation must preserve for specified future decisions.**

## Governing direction

Bellman is the mathematical research track for Writ. Its purpose is to assemble a cumulative mathematical substrate for consequential decisions using established mathematics and, where genuinely encountered, new mathematics.

The goal is not to force every domain into one equation. The goal is to identify clean, durable mathematical structures—probability, information, causality, decision, dynamics, equivalence, strategy, uncertainty, and related structures—and make their assumptions and interfaces precise enough that they can be composed, checked, reused, and corrected over time.

For each mathematical component, Bellman should make explicit:

1. **Object** — what mathematical thing is being represented.
2. **Assumptions** — under what conditions the mathematics is valid.
3. **Operation** — what transformation, inference, comparison, or decision is permitted.
4. **Guarantee** — what follows if the assumptions hold.
5. **Composition rule** — when the result can safely be combined with other mathematical objects or results.
6. **Failure boundary** — when reuse or composition is no longer justified.
7. **Provenance** — what evidence, model, theorem, computation, or assumption the result depends on.

## Relationship to engineering

Bellman defines mathematical meaning and valid composition. Writ engineering makes those structures executable, interoperable, versioned, checkable, reusable, and correctable.

The two tracks should develop in parallel against the same substrate:

**Bellman:** objects → assumptions → operations → guarantees → composition rules.

**Engineering:** encode → compute → check → version → compose → reuse → correct.

Neither track should silently outrun the other. Engineering must not claim a mathematical guarantee Bellman has not specified. Bellman should not require a bespoke infrastructure before a mathematical operation has earned implementation.

## Discovery discipline

Bellman may freely adopt and combine established mathematics. Mathematical novelty is not required for progress.

New mathematical experiments are appropriate when a real unresolved question emerges, but the programme should not manufacture novelty by repeatedly changing grids, summaries, or assumptions after a result closes.

A stopped experiment remains cumulative knowledge. A known theorem can become part of Bellman's substrate when its assumptions, guarantees, and composition rules are made explicit and usable.

## Long-term orientation

The ambition can remain very large while the first demonstrated competence stays narrow and exact. Global affairs, war, security, biology, science, or other domains are future stress environments for transferable mathematical structure—not reasons to weaken the mathematics.

Bellman should become progressively better at answering:

> **What distinctions matter for the future decisions we care about, what can safely be discarded, what guarantees survive reuse and combination, and how do we know?**
